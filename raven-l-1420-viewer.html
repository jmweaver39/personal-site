Overview

The Raven L-1420 payload is a self-contained avionics and camera bay flown on NCSSM Rocketry's high-power airframe. Everything lives on a 3D-printed sled between plywood bulkheads: flight computer, radios, sensors, cameras and power, sized to slide into the booster's payload tube.

Electronics

  • Orange Pi 3B — flight computer, runs capture and logging
  • RFD900x — 915 MHz telemetry link to the ground station
  • GPS tracker + LoRa — position downlink and long-range backup
  • BMP388 / ENS160 — barometric altitude and air quality
  • Three cameras — two side-view flight cams and a slow-mo unit
  • 24 V → 5 V DC-DC — 5 A supply off the flight battery

Structure

The dark gray body parts are printed; bulkheads are pine plywood with brass heat-set inserts and black M3 hardware throughout. A steel U-bolt on the forward bulkhead takes the recovery harness.
